The 10-minute walking catchment around Crossharbour contains an estimated 8,016 residents, with an average density of 117.8 residents per hectare across approximately 68 hectares of analytical land. 3 schools are identified within the catchment, including 2 primary and 1 secondary schools. The catchment follows the pedestrian network rather than extending evenly from the site. Docks and the River Thames constrain east–west movement, while connections extend primarily north–south through the Isle of Dogs. For the emerging masterplan, this highlights the importance of connecting the site to surrounding neighbourhoods, schools and community infrastructure. Five- and 15-minute catchments provide reference scenarios. Alternative population allocations test sensitivity to where residents are located within census areas. Population is estimated from Census 2021, not a present-day headcount. The model represents existing walking connections from two proxy access points, not confirmed future entrances. School locations indicate spatial proximity, not capacity or admissions; pedestrian gates remain unverified.
